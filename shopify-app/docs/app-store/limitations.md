# UpsellTrix — Limitation Disclosures (merchant-friendly)

Use these phrases in the App Store listing, reviewer instructions, and in-app help.

---

## Accelerated Checkout / Buy it now

**Accelerated Checkout compatibility: Not currently supported.**

Free gifts unlock when shoppers use Add to cart and continue through the cart to checkout. If a shopper uses Buy it now or another Accelerated Checkout button on the product page, they skip the cart and automatic gifts are not added for that path.

Supported: Add to cart → cart / cart drawer → checkout.

---

## Theme compatibility

UpsellTrix is verified on Dawn and Savor. Other themes may not be supported. Use Theme setup and Store health check after enabling the embed.

---

## Theme app embed required

The progress bar appears only after the UpsellTrix theme app embed is enabled and saved on the theme you are testing. Draft and live themes are separate.

---

## Online Store required

UpsellTrix promotions display on the Shopify Online Store. Custom storefronts without the theme embed / App Proxy path are outside the supported setup.

---

## Third-party cart drawers

Some third-party drawers work; others may need theme adjustments. If the progress bar does not appear in a drawer, check Theme setup, confirm the embed is on, and use Store health check.

---

## Campaign types

Available now: Spend X → Free Gift and Tiered Free Gifts.  
Mystery Gift is shown as coming soon and cannot be created yet.

---

## Discount sync lag (rare)

Campaign changes save immediately. Shopify discount synchronization usually completes quickly; if gifts look wrong for a minute after activation, wait and refresh the storefront, then check Store health.
