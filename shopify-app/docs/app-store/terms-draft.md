## 1. Service description

UpsellTrix is a Shopify application that lets merchants create and manage spend-threshold free-gift promotions (including Spend X → Free Gift and Tiered Free Gifts) and display related progress UI on supported Online Store themes via a theme app embed.

---

## 2. Eligibility and Shopify dependency

You must have a valid Shopify store and accept Shopify’s terms. UpsellTrix depends on Shopify APIs, webhooks, Online Store, and theme app extensions. We are not Shopify. Shopify platform outages or API changes may affect the App.

---

## 3. Merchant responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- Configuring campaigns accurately (thresholds, gifts, messaging)  
- Enabling the theme app embed on themes you intend to use  
- Ensuring gift products/variants are available for sale where required  
- Complying with advertising, consumer, tax, and discount laws in your jurisdictions  
- Reviewing storefront behavior before launching promotions

## 6. Limitations of the service

Without limiting other disclosures:

- Online Store and theme app embed are required for storefront promotions  
- Theme compatibility is not universal; Dawn and Savor are the validated themes  
- Accelerated Checkout / Buy it now paths are not supported for automatic gift insertion  
- Discount synchronization may occasionally lag after campaign changes
