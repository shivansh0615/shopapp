## App testing information

**Test account**

Select **My app doesn't require an account to use it**. UpsellTrix uses the Shopify installation and does not have a separate login. Do not enter test credentials.

**Screencast URL**

Leave blank until the reviewer video is uploaded. Paste its accessible unlisted URL here.

**Testing instructions**

1. Install UpsellTrix on a test store with the Online Store channel and approve the requested permissions. No separate UpsellTrix login is required.
2. Open UpsellTrix and confirm the Overview page loads.
3. Open Theme setup. In the theme editor, enable UpsellTrix under App embeds and save the theme. Dawn or another Online Store 2.0 theme is recommended.
4. Open Campaigns, select Create campaign, and choose Spend X → Free Gift.
5. Set a reachable threshold. Choose an available gift product and variant that are published to the Online Store. Keep the supported product and cart placements enabled, then activate the campaign.
6. Open the storefront and use the standard Add to cart flow. Add regular merchandise below the threshold and confirm the progress experience appears.
7. Add more merchandise until the threshold is reached. Confirm the eligible gift appears in the cart and is free on the supported checkout path.
8. Reduce the cart below the threshold and confirm the gift is removed.
9. Create a Tiered Free Gifts campaign with at least two increasing thresholds and available gift variants. Activate it and confirm gifts unlock as spend increases.
10. Pause the campaign and confirm the promotion stops.

Notes:
- Automatic gift insertion supports Add to cart → cart or drawer → checkout.
- Buy it now and accelerated checkout gift insertion are not supported.
- Mystery Gift is marked Coming soon and is intentionally not selectable.
- The theme app embed must be enabled on the exact theme being tested.
